- Designed and assembled an autonomous robot, using Fusion 360 for CAD design and an Arduino Adafruit Feather 32u4 for motor control and sensor integration, resulting in a functional prototype capable of navigating obstacle courses, securing 3rd place in a university competition.
- Implemented electrical wiring and sensor integration, combining ultrasonic sensors and motor drivers, achieving a response time of less than 100ms for obstacle detection and avoidance.
