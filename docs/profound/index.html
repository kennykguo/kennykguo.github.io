Searching for the secrets to the world. My favourite pieces of writing/content.

  • The War of Art

    Steven Pressfield

    An anthropomorphic way of describing struggles in the creative process. "If you were the last person on earth, would you still do it?".

  • How to Read a Book

    Mortimer J. Adler and Charles Van Doren

    I think everybody should read this haha.

  • Teardrop

    Massive Attack

    Song lyrics are melancholic, and the beat mimics a heartbeat. But, I feel hopeful, and empowered. There is a challenge ahead, that awaits. "Fearless on my, breath".

  • Zeroth-Principles Thinking

    Bryan Johnson

    If everything which you desired disappeared and all your needs were met, what would you do?

  • The Scaling Hypothesis

    Gwern

    "All problems in computer science can be solved by another level of indirection" (layers of software)

  • Man's Search for Meaning

    Viktor E. Frankl

    Very deep introspectional book, coming from a person to which the world took everything away from him. "Those who have a 'why' to live, can bear with almost any 'how"

  • reactive agency

    Claire Wang

    Guilty of reactive agency. Figuring out your values is difficult!

  • Design is the practice of seeing through the surface of things to understand their underlying structure, then rearranging those elements into new forms that didn't exist before

    Ryo Lu

    Break things, then build them back up.

  • Vagabond

    Takehiko Inoue

    Journey of a young samurai as he finds his purpose killing people with his sword.

  • Make Something Wonderful

    Steve Jobs

    Put something back into the pool of human experience.

  • Final Performance

    Whiplash

    What is greatness like behind closed doors?

  • I'm Coming Back for Everything

    The Social Network

    You can feel the betrayal through the present/past storytelling, and music timing.

  • The Complete Mathematics of Neural Networks and Deep Learning

    Adam Dhalla

    Rabbithole into machine learning started here.
